Common warning signs

  • Pressure to act immediately or threats of account closure.
  • Requests for passwords, one-time codes or payment.
  • Unexpected attachments or shortened links.
  • Sender addresses or domains that imitate trusted brands.
  • Offers that seem unusually generous or unrealistic.
